## Did induction heads form?

**No.** No head reaches a prefix-matching score of 0.5 at any checkpoint: the best at the end of training is 0.031 (L3.H3), against 0.014 for evenly spread attention, and the loss on the second copy is hardly lower than on the first (10.84 vs 11.44). The model has not learned to copy from earlier context.

- Largest jump in the best prefix-matching score: between step 3,130 and step 4,681 (0.024 -> 0.034).
- Best induction head at the end of training: L3.H3 (score 0.031); previous-token heads at the end: none above threshold.
- First checkpoint with a previous-token score >= 0.5: none; the best previous-token score grows from 0.043 to 0.237 (previous-token heads are the first half of an induction circuit).
- Repeat loss on the second copy goes from 11.24 to 11.03 across the formation interval (first copy stays near 9.86, the cost of guessing a random token).
- Validation loss falls 0.442 per unit of ln(step + 1) in the formation interval, vs 0.388 in the neighbouring intervals (ratio 1.14).

Rule fixed in advance: a ratio of at least 2 counts as a sharp drop coinciding with induction-head formation. **Result: no.** Since no induction head formed, the interval only tracks a small change and the test has little meaning here; it is reported because it was planned.

Resolution is limited by the checkpoints: 23 log-spaced steps, so a formation interval can span a factor of about 1.5 in steps.
