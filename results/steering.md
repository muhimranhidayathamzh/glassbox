# Milestone 4: steering with SAE features

Generated by `python -m glassbox.steer`. Do not edit by hand.

A vector alpha x 19.5 x u is added to the residual stream entering block 2 at every position >= 1, where 19.5 is the mean residual norm there and u is a unit vector: the decoder direction of the concept's SAE feature, or one of 4 random directions. Each condition: 48 stories (3 prompts x 16 seeds, the same seeds in every condition), up to 60 new tokens, temperature 0.8, top-k 50.

- **mention rate**: share of stories that contain the concept word (or its plural)
- **NLL**: loss of the generated continuation under the unsteered model, in nats/token; higher means less natural text

Concept words, the feature-selection rule, alphas, prompts and seeds were fixed before any result was seen. Feature rule: the live feature whose automatic label is the word's token itself, with at least 10 distinct examples; highest label share wins.
